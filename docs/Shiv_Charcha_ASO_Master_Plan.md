# Shiv Charcha — Google Play ASO & Store Conversion Master Plan

**Product:** Shiv Charcha  
**Publisher:** Mahavyoma Studio  
**Primary market:** India, especially Hindi-speaking users  
**Primary audience:** Shiv Charcha / Shiv Guru devotees, rural and semi-urban users, older users, users with limited literacy, and users who want deeper reading/learning  
**Platform priority:** Android first, with the product also available cross-platform

> This document is the working Google Play ASO, conversion, creative, localization, experimentation, and launch plan for Shiv Charcha V1.

---

## 1. Core positioning

Do **not** position the app as another generic “Lord Shiva wallpaper / bhajan app.”

The strongest positioning is:

> **A complete Shiv Charcha devotional experience — listen, learn, practice, experience and share.**

The app combines:

- Shiv Charcha / Shiv Guru knowledge
- Audio-first explanations
- Bhajans
- 108 Jap
- Interactive devotional experiences
- Shiva stories and mythology
- Jyotirlingas
- Shakti Peethas
- Shiva temples and pilgrimage content
- Important dates and calendar
- Wallpapers
- Devotional sounds / ringtone functionality where supported
- Share Studio
- Personalized devotional cards
- Favorites and personal history
- User-selectable visual themes

### The positioning rule

**Shiv Charcha is the identity.  
Shiv Sansar is the breadth.  
Devotional experiences are the emotional hook.  
Shareability is the growth loop.**

# 4. Short description strategy

The short description is one of the first pieces of copy users see.

It should communicate **what makes the app different**, not attempt to list every feature.

### Recommended primary short description

**शिव चर्चा, शिव गुरु, भजन, 108 जाप, पूजा, कैलेंडर और भक्तिमय सामग्री**

Approximate positioning:

- Shiv Charcha
- Shiv Guru
- Bhajan
- 108 Jap
- devotional content

### Alternative versions to test

**शिव गुरु से जुड़ें, भजन सुनें, 108 जाप करें और शिव चर्चा सीखें**

**शिव चर्चा का भक्तिमय साथी — ज्ञान, भजन, जाप, पूजा और कैलेंडर**

Do not publish all three simultaneously. Use experiments later.

Avoid keyword stuffing.

Google explicitly says unnecessary keyword repetition does not improve ranking and can create a poor user experience.

# 7. Hindi localization strategy

Hindi should NOT be treated as a machine-translated afterthought.

Create a genuinely Hindi-first listing.

Use:

- simple Hindi
- familiar terms
- natural Hindi sentence structure
- limited unnecessary Sanskrit complexity
- clear devotional vocabulary

The target user may read slowly or may prefer audio.

The store listing should communicate that the app supports listening and interaction.

### Example Hindi positioning

**सुनें • सीखें • जाप करें • भक्ति अनुभव करें • साझा करें**

Use this as visual creative language rather than stuffing it into metadata.

# 9. Store icon strategy

## Recommended icon concept

A recognizable, premium **Shivling-based symbol** with a subtle divine glow and a clean abstract trishul/rising-flame element.

Do NOT put:

- app name
- small text
- long words
- slogans
- “free”
- stars
- badges
- download arrows
- rating claims

### Icon creative direction

- Deep plum / maroon background
- Rich saffron
- Warm gold
- Ivory highlights
- Strong central Shivling silhouette
- Soft divine halo
- Clean silhouette
- Premium semi-3D or polished illustrative depth

Create separate master/production assets for:

- Google Play icon
- Android adaptive foreground/background
- monochrome variant
- iOS icon

### Icon test

At minimum preview at:

- 1024px
- 192px
- 96px
- 64px
- 48px

If the symbol disappears at 48px, simplify it.

# 12. Screenshot design rules

Every screenshot should have:

- one dominant message
- minimal copy
- real app UI
- beautiful devotional artwork
- strong visual contrast
- consistent typography
- consistent framing
- consistent device presentation

Google Play advises using screenshots to highlight the actual in-app experiences that make the app special and to keep text minimal.

# 38. Store listing trust rules

Never use:

- fake testimonials
- fake download counts
- fake ratings
- “official” without authorization
- invented endorsements
- unsupported historical claims
- fabricated quotes

Never present an unofficial image/audio/story as official.

Keep metadata factual.

Google Play's store listing policies apply to all localized versions.

# 39. ASO copywriting rules

Write naturally.

Good:

> शिव गुरु से जुड़ें और हर दिन सुनें, सीखें, जाप करें और साझा करें।

Bad:

> Shiv Charcha Shiv Guru Shiv Charcha app Shiv Guru bhajan Shiv Charcha Shiv Guru 108 Jap Shiv wallpaper

The second version is keyword stuffing and damages trust.

# 45. Final ASO philosophy

Do not try to win by having the most keywords.

Do not try to win by claiming the app is “best.”

Do not try to win by stuffing screenshots with text.

Win by making the Play Store listing communicate something compelling in the first few seconds:

# शिव चarcha सिर्फ पढ़ने के लिए नहीं है।

# सुनें।
# सीखें।
# करें।
# महसूस करें।
# साझा करें।

The icon gets recognition.

The feature graphic creates curiosity.

The first three screenshots establish differentiation.

The remaining screenshots prove breadth.

The description builds trust.

The product itself delivers the promise.

Then use Play Console experiments and custom store listings to learn what actually converts.
